# Cyber Security Escape Room VR: The Northwind Breach

A story-driven VR cyber-security game for Meta Quest (Unity 6, URP, Meta XR SDK). You are a new **blue-team analyst** on your first night shift. Maya, your SOC lead, guides you over the radio through three chapters, from spotting the alert to closing the incident.

| | Chapter | Where | What you do |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Night Shift** | SOC control room | Learn the controls, open the SIEM alert, read the logs, identify the attack, escalate, and badge through the door |
| 2 | **Fred's Office** | Floor 3 | Escape room against the clock: evidence, shredding, USB bait, password guessing and reset, 2FA, phishing, social engineering, encryption, and the door code |
| 3 | **The Server Room** | Basement data centre | Find and unplug the rogue implant, bag it, quarantine its switch port, restore a clean backup, and file the incident report |

The full story takes about 25–40 minutes and teaches 22 learning points. Progress, best times and learning points are saved between sessions.

## Run it
**Meta XR Simulator:** open `Assets/_Project/Scenes/MainMenu.unity`, enable the Simulator (Meta XR toolbar) and press Play.
**Quest 3:** *File > Build Profiles > Meta Quest > Switch Platform*, fix "Required" items in *Meta > Tools > Project Setup Tool*, then *Build And Run*.
**Rebuild everything:** *CSER > Build All Scenes (story mode)* regenerates and bakes all four scenes from code (a few minutes on a GPU).

## Controls
| Action | Controllers | Hands | Simulator |
|---|---|---|---|
| Use screens/buttons | Ray + **trigger** | Pinch, or poke with a finger | Left click |
| Grab | **Grip** (point + grip pulls items to you) | Grab | Grip key |
| Move | Left stick: teleport; right stick: turn | Walk | WASD, right mouse to look |
| Menu & hints | **Y / B**, or MENU/HINT on the left wrist | Tap MENU/HINT on the wrist | M key |

Guidance: Maya's voice and subtitles, a green marker over the next object (it moves to the drop spot while you carry an item), the objective on your wrist, and three hints per objective (nudge, then direction, then the answer).

## Tech notes
- **Scenes are generated by code** (`Assets/_Project/Scripts/Editor/EscapeRoomBuilder.cs` + `StoryBuilder.cs`), so they're reproducible. Don't hand-edit them.
- Interaction uses the Meta Interaction SDK Quick Actions (comprehensive rig; ray and poke canvases; grab and distance grab; teleport hotspots).
- Lighting is baked (area lights, AO, light probes, a box-projected reflection probe) plus one mixed spot for held-object shadows. Post-processing (ACES, bloom, grading) runs on PC; on Quest it is switched off and foveated rendering is on (`PlatformTuning.cs`).
- **No flicker:** camera near/far is 0.05 m / 60 m for depth-buffer precision, and labels and decals sit at least 1 mm off their surfaces.
- Voice-over: `Tools/generate_vo.ps1` regenerates every line in `Resources/VO/narration.json` using Windows speech synthesis.
